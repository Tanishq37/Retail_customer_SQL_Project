# Retail Customer Insights Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

This project analyzed 3,900 retail customer transactions to understand purchasing behavior, revenue trends, customer preferences, and high-value customer segments. The analysis was conducted using SQL, covering data cleaning, exploratory analysis, revenue analysis, customer analysis, advanced SQL techniques, and customer segmentation.

## Dataset Overview

* Total Records: 3,900
* Missing Values: 0
* Duplicate Records: 0
* Customer Age Range: 18–70 years
* Product Categories: Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, Outerwear

## Key Findings

### Revenue Performance

* Total revenue generated was **$233,081**.
* Average purchase value was **$59.76**.
* Purchase amounts ranged from **$20 to $100**.
* **Clothing** was the highest revenue-generating category, contributing **$104,264**.
* **Fall** was the best-performing season with **$60,018** in revenue.
* **Montana** generated the highest revenue among all locations.

### Customer Insights

* Male customers accounted for the majority of sales revenue (**$157,890**).
* Female customers had a slightly higher average spend per purchase (**$60.25**) compared to males (**$59.54**).
* The average customer age was **44 years**.
* Customers aged **49 years** generated the highest total spending.

### Product & Shopping Behavior

* Clothing was the most popular product category with **1,737 purchases**.
* Jewelry was the most frequently purchased item.
* Average customer review rating was **3.75/5**, indicating generally positive customer satisfaction.
* PayPal was the most preferred payment method.
* Customers purchasing every three months generated the highest revenue among all purchase frequency groups.

### Subscription & Discount Analysis

* Non-subscribers generated **$170,436** in revenue compared to **$62,645** from subscribers.
* Average spending was nearly identical for subscribers and non-subscribers.
* Purchases without discounts generated more revenue than discounted purchases.
* Customers purchasing without discounts spent slightly more on average.

### High-Value Customer Analysis

* Several customers achieved the maximum purchase amount of **$100**.
* The most loyal customers recorded **50 previous purchases**.
* High-value customers were identified using a customer value score based on spending and purchase frequency.
* These customers represent an important segment for retention and loyalty programs.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase investment in the Clothing category, as it consistently drives the highest revenue across all seasons.
2. Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of the Fall season to maximize sales opportunities.
3. Develop loyalty and subscription programs to improve customer retention and increase recurring purchases.
4. Focus retention efforts on identified high-value customers through personalized rewards and exclusive offers.
5. Promote preferred payment methods such as PayPal and Credit Card to maintain a smooth customer experience.
6. Implement targeted marketing campaigns for age groups and customer segments with the highest spending potential.
7. Evaluate discount strategies carefully, as non-discounted purchases currently generate higher revenue.
8. Use customer segmentation insights to create personalized promotions and improve engagement.

## Conclusion

The analysis revealed that Clothing is the primary revenue driver, Fall is the strongest sales season, and high-value customers contribute significantly to business performance. The findings provide actionable insights that can help improve customer retention, optimize marketing efforts, and support future revenue growth.
